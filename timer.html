5 Min Timer

Five minutes is short enough that starting doesn't feel like a decision.

What's this for?

Putting something off is usually about starting, not the work itself. The deal is simple: promise yourself five minutes and nothing more. Five minutes is small enough that your brain won't argue.

Almost every time, you'll keep going once the timer runs out — but if you don't, that's fine. You still did five minutes more than nothing. Set it, press start, and work until it stops.
